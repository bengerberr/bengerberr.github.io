Card Club privacy

Card Club is a free card game site for friends and family, run by Ben Gerber. Last updated 9 November 2026.

Playing without signing in

You can play without an account. We keep the name you type, your moves, and any chat messages for that game room. Rooms are deleted after a day of not being used.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you do, Google tells Card Club your name, email address, profile photo and an account ID. We use them to:

  • remember your name and give you your seat back if you change phones,
  • keep your game stats (rounds played, wins and so on),
  • run your friends list, so your friends can see when you're in a game and join you.

Other players see your display name, photo and friend code. They never see your email address. We don't get access to anything else in your Google account, and we don't use your information for ads.

Where it's kept

Everything is stored in Google Apps Script, in the Google account that runs Card Club. It isn't sold or shared with anyone else.

Deleting your information

Sign out any time from your profile. To have your account, stats and friends list deleted, ask Ben and it will be removed. You can also remove Card Club's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.
